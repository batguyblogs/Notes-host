# 15/09/26 Tue 10:37am

### Dynamics of Chemical Systems

#### Isothermal CSTR (Continuous Stirred Tank Reactor)

>[!important] Mass balance equation
>$\text{Rate of Change of Mass accumulation}=\text{In-Out+Generation-Consumption}$

![[Assets/L17 2026-09-15 10.45.16.excalidraw\|600]]
$$
C_{A} = \text{Concentration of A in reactor}
$$
$$
C_{B} = \text{Concentration of B in reactor}
$$
$$
C_{Ai}=\text{Inlet concentration of A}
$$
$$
F = \text{Volumetric flow rate} \left( \frac{m^{3}}{s} \right)
$$
$$
V = \text{reactor volume}
$$
$$
K =  \text{reaction rate constant}
$$
$$
r = KC_{A} = \text{Reaction rate per unit volume} \frac{mol}{m^{3}s}
$$
$$
\boxed{V \frac{\delta C_{A}}{\delta t}=F.C_{Ai} - F.C_{A}+0-K.C_{A}.V}
$$
$$
\boxed{
V.\frac{\delta C_{B}}{\delta t}=0-F.C_{B}+K.C_{A}.V-0
}
$$
$$
\frac{\delta C_{A}}{\delta t}=\frac{F.C_{Ai}}{V}-K.C_{A}
$$
$$
\frac{\delta C_{A}}{\delta t}= \frac{F.C_{Ai}}{V}-\frac{FC_{A}}{V}-K.C_{A}
$$
$$
\frac{\delta C_{B}}{\delta t}=K.C_{A}-\frac{F.C_{B}}{V}
$$
$\implies$
$$
\frac{\delta C_{A}}{\delta t}= \frac{F}{V}(C_{Ai}-C_{A})-KC_{A}
$$
$$
\frac{\delta C_{B}}{\delta t}=K.C_{A}-\frac{F.C_{B}}{V}
$$
Now to model it in state space
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
\dot{C_{A}} \\
\dot{C_{B}}
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
-K -\dfrac{F}{V} & 0 \\
K & -\dfrac{F}{V}
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
C_{A} \\
C_{B}
\end{bmatrix}
+\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{F}{V} \\
0
\end{bmatrix}
C_{Ai}
$$
and the output will be
$$
Y=C_{B}
$$
