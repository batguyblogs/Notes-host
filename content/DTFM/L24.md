## Q1
Assume that each neuron has sigmoidal activation function. Perform a forward pass and backward pass on the network. Assume that actual output is 0.5 and learning rate $\eta$ is 1

x1 = 0.35
x2=0.9
w13 = 0.1
w14= 0.4
w23=0.8
w24=0.6
nodes H3 (y3)
H4 (y4)
w35 = 0.3
w45 = 0.9 

### Solution
Forward Pass Computation
$$
net_{3} = 0.35 \times 0.1 + 0.9 \times 0.8 = 0.755
$$
$$
y_{3}=\frac{1}{1+e^{-net_{3}}} = 0.68
$$
$$
net_{4} = 0.35 \times 0.4 + 0.9 \times 0.6 = 0.68
$$
$$
y_{4} = 0.66
$$
$$
net_{5} = y_{3} \times_{0}.3 + y_{4} \times 0.9
$$
$$
y_{5}=0.69
$$
---
Back Propagation

$$
e=\hat{y}-y=0.5-0.69=-0.19
$$
$$
\frac{\delta E}{\delta w_{35}}=-(\hat{y}-y)(y_{5})(1-y_{5})\times y_{3}
$$
>[!note]-
>$$
>-e \ (\text{the error}) \\ 
> (y_{n})(1-y_{n}) \ (\text{the differentiation for the sigmoidal activation function}) \\
> \text{and the value of the node} 
>$$
