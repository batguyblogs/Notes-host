## Activation Functions
### 1) Linear Activation Function
$$
Y = f(u) = 
\begin{Bmatrix}
u, & u>0 \\
0, & u<0
\end{Bmatrix}
$$
### 2) Sigmoid Activation Function
$$
f(u)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-u}}
$$
### 3) tanh Activation Function
$$
f(u) = \frac{e^x - e^{-x}}{e^x+e^{-x}}=\frac{2}{1+e^{-2x}}
$$
### 4) ReLU (Rectified Linear Unit)
$$
f(u)=max
\begin{Bmatrix}
0, & x
\end{Bmatrix}
$$
### 5) leaky ReLU
$$
f(u)=
\begin{Bmatrix}
x, & x\geq_{0} \\
\alpha x, & x<0
\end{Bmatrix}
$$
for $0<\alpha\ll1$ 
### 6) Soft Plus
$$
f(x)=\log(1+e^x)
$$
### 7) ELU
$$
f(x)=
\begin{Bmatrix}
x, & x\geq0 \\
e^x-1, & x<0
\end{Bmatrix}
$$
#### 7.1) Scaled ELU
$$
f(x)=
\begin{Bmatrix}
x, & x\geq0 \\
\alpha(e^x-1), & x<0
\end{Bmatrix}
$$
for $0<\alpha\ll1$

## Artifical Neural Network Update Using Backpropagation and Gradient Decent
Assuming the activation function for each neuron is the sigmoid function.

$$
\sigma = \frac{1}{1+e^{-x}}
$$
$$
\frac{\delta \sigma}{\delta x} = \frac{e^{-x}}{(1+e^{-x})^{2}}
$$
$$
\frac{\delta\sigma}{\delta x}= \frac{1}{1+e^{-x}}.\frac{e^{-x}}{1+e^{-x}}
$$
$$
\implies \frac{\delta\sigma}{\delta x}=\sigma (1-\sigma)
$$
