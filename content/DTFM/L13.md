### Mathematical Modelling of Electrical Circuits

| Classification  | Function            | Application  |

| Inverter        | DC-AC               | Solar PV, EV |
| Buck Converter  | DC-DC<br>Vin > Vout | EV           |
| Boost Converter | DC-DC<br>Vin < Vout |              |
| Buck - Boost    | DC - DC             |              |
##### Buck Converter
$$
V_{out}=D\times V_{in}
$$
![[Assets/L13 2026-09-16 19.44.06.excalidraw\|600]]

1. Switch is on, diode is reverse biased

![[Assets/L13 2026-09-17 08.58.05.excalidraw\|600]]

Current across inductor

$$
V_{L}=L \frac{\delta I}{\delta t}
$$
applying KCL

$$
0 = V_{i} - V_{L} -V_{o}
$$

$$
V_{L}=V_{i}-V_{o}
$$

$$
V_{i}-V_{o}=L \frac{\delta I_{L}}{\delta t}
$$

$$
V_{i}-V_{o}=L \frac{\Delta I_{L}}{\Delta T}
$$

$$
D = \frac{T_{ON}}{T_{ON}+T_{OFF}} \implies D=\frac{T_{ON}}{T} \implies T_{ON}=D.T
$$

$$
V_{i}-V_{o}=L \frac{\Delta I_{L}}{D.T}
$$

$$
\Delta I_{L}= \frac{V_{i}-V_{o}}{L} \times D.T
$$
2. Switch is off, Diode is forward biased

![[Assets/L13 2026-09-17 09.46.04.excalidraw\|600]]

$$
-V_{L}-V_{o}=0
$$

$$
V_{L}+V_{o}=0
$$

$$
V_{L}=-V_{0}
$$

$$
L \frac{\delta I_{L}}{\delta t}=-V_{0}
$$

$$
L \frac{\Delta I_{L}}{\Delta T}=-V_{0}
$$

$$
\frac{T_{ON}}{T_{ON}+T_{OFF}}=0
$$

$$
1- \frac{T_{ON}}{T_{ON}+T_{OFF}}=1-D
$$

$$
\frac{T_{OFF}}{T}=1-D
$$

$$
T_{OFF}=(1-D)T
$$
Substituting, we get
$$
\Delta I_{L} = -\frac{V_{0}(1-D)}{L}T
$$
$$
-\frac{V_{0}(1-D)T}{L}+\frac{(V_{i}-V_{0})DT}{L}=0
$$
$$
-V_{0}(1-D)T+\frac{(V_{i}-V_{o})DT}{L}=0
$$
$$
-V_{0}(1-D)T+(V_{i}-V_{0})DT=0
$$
$$
V_{0}=V_{i}.D
$$
